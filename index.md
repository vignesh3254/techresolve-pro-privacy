# TechResolve Pro Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 19, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

TechResolve Pro is a social media workflow and automation application designed to help its operator create, review, schedule, publish, and analyze IT-related content for connected social media accounts.

This policy explains what information the application processes and how it is handled.

## 2. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled, TechResolve Pro may process:

- Account information provided by connected platforms, such as account identifiers and profile details.
- Authentication tokens and connection information required to access authorized platform features.
- Drafts, captions, images, hashtags, approval decisions, and scheduled publishing details.
- Publishing status, error logs, and available post analytics.
- Application security and operational logs.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information is used to connect authorized accounts, manage content, execute approved publishing workflows, diagnose errors, maintain application security, and display available analytics.

Content is intended to be published only to accounts authorized by the operator and according to the application's publishing settings.

## 4. Authentication and Security

Platform authentication tokens are handled by the application for authorized integrations. The application is designed to protect stored credentials using encryption. Access should be restricted to authorized users.

No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Third-Party Platforms

When an account is connected, the relevant platform may process information according to its own terms and privacy policy.

- Instagram and Meta: https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy/
- LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy

TechResolve Pro is not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta or LinkedIn.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained as needed to operate the application, maintain publishing history, and meet legitimate operational or legal requirements.

To request deletion of information held by the operator, contact the email address below. You can also disconnect an account and revoke the application's access through the relevant platform settings. Removing a connection may not automatically delete information already retained in application backups or logs.

## 7. Sharing

Information is sent to connected platforms when needed to perform an authorized action, such as publishing content or retrieving analytics. Information may also be processed by hosting or infrastructure providers used to operate the application.

TechResolve Pro does not intend to sell personal information.

## 8. Children's Privacy

TechResolve Pro is not intended to collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
